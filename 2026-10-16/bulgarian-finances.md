Sources: Google News RSS (bg), bta.bg, bnrnews.bg, bntnews.bg, minfin.bg, investor.bg/SEGA/paragraf headlines via RSS.

**Government tables €660 minimum wage for 2027.** Draft Cabinet decree published Thursday on the public-consultation portal: МРЗ rises from €620.20 to €660 (+6.4%) on 1 Jan 2027, i.e. €12.80 below the new formula's €672.80 ceiling; the hourly rate moves to €3.98.
