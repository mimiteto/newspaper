**Budget-discipline caps are being deleted, not tightened.** A government ЗИД на Закона за публичните финанси repeals чл. 23 and чл. 24 ЗПФ — the provisions that hold the medium-term structural-deficit objective at 0.5% of GDP, tolerating more than 0.5% but never above 1% of GDP and only where general-government debt is under 40% of GDP with low long-term sustainability risk; the bill was tabled by МС on 26 October and the budget committee takes it on first reading the week after the runoff, which "Демократична България"'s Владислав Панев calls "два дни след балотажа".
https://www.segabg.com/hot/category-economy/vlastta-gotvi-opasno-razhlabvane-na-byudzhetnata-disciplina
https://www.bia-bg.com/dialog/view/arc/22462/

**The tax package — including the phased ДДС threshold — still is not in parliament.** БСК's finance-bill tracker (discussion opened 26.10, run until 12.11) lists as tabled only the ЗПФ and Фискален съвет amendments from МС (26.10) and the Валутен закон amendment (20.10); the only ЗДДС bills are opposition ones (Възраждане, 23 September), so the finance ministry package — mandatory VAT registration threshold €51,130 → €60,000 from 1 January 2027, €70,000 from 2028, €75,000 from 2029 — has not been filed, and the parliament's own bills feed shows no registration newer than 26 October.

**МРЗ 2027 is adopted: €660 a month under Постановление № 235 на МС.** The ordinance "относно определяне размера на минималната работна заплата за страната /660 евро/" was issued on 29 October and posted to the Council of Ministers act register on 30 October — €660 gross for a full month (+€39.80, +6.4% on 2026's €620.20) from 1 January 2027, on the legal ground of чл. 244, ал. 1, т. 1 КТ, amending ПП № 129/2012 and ПП № 66/1996; the МТСП text carries the hourly minimum at €3.98.

**NOI's 2027 retirement parameters land 1 November.** Required insurance seniority for the general regime peaks on 1 January 2027: women need 62 years 8 months of age (+2 months) with 37 years of seniority (+2 months), men 64 years 10 months (+1 month) with 40 years (+2 months), the cohorts notified being women born 1 July 1964 – 30 April 1965 and men born 1 April 1962 – 28 February 1963; retiring early still costs 0.4% of the pension for life per month of early exit, the чл. 68, ал. 3 КСО fallback stays at 67 years plus 15 years of actual seniority, and the 2027 poverty line is €443 (+13.4%).
