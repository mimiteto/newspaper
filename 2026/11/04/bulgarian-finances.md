# Bulgaria — economic & labour-law news, last 2 days (03–04 Nov 2026)

Sources: Google News RSS (`hl=bg&gl=BG&ceid=BG:bg`, `when:2d` queries on МРЗ / Кодекс на труда / ЗЧРБ / ДДС праг / осигурителен доход / Бюджет 2027 / Държавен вестник), plus strategy.bg, bnrnews.bg (БНР), banker.bg, marica.bg, standartnews.com, plovdiv24.bg, dunavmost.com, business.dir.bg, paragraph21.com, bnrnews/noi.bg, mlsp.government.bg, tretavazrast.com, northpoint.bg and focus-news.net.

**ДДС / VAT registration threshold.** Finance re-planned the mandatory VAT registration threshold as a three-step climb — €60,000 from 1 Jan 2027, €70,000 in 2028, €75,000 in 2029 — after EC/IMF advice that a one-off jump from the current €51,130 would cost revenue; on 3 Nov Petar Vitanov said the Commission still has reservations (he describes it as "€50,000 → €70,000") and that Budget 2027 will only be tabled on Friday 6 Nov.
- https://banker.bg/2026-11-03/petar-vitanov-byudzhet-prag-po-dds/
- https://www.northpoint.bg/post/pragat-registratsia-dds-raste-tri-stapki-60-000-70-000-75

**Budget 2027 / deficit.** The 2027 draft — Bulgaria's first as a euro-area member — went to the Commission and Eurogroup on 15 Oct, is now five days late (6 Nov), while the country has been in an excessive-deficit procedure since June with a 2025 deficit of 4.2%; Brussels' verdict is expected 24 Nov.
- https://banker.bg/2026-11-03/petar-vitanov-byudzhet-prag-po-dds/

**МРЗ / minimum wage is final.** The Council of Ministers' Ordinance № 235 of 29 Oct 2026 was published on 3 Nov (State Gazette issue 100), fixing the minimum wage at €660 a month / €3.98 an hour from 1 Jan 2027 — up €39.80 (6.4%) on the current €620.20; Minister Efremova says it covers ~462,000 workers and beats 5.7% annual inflation, while the 23 Oct tripartite session ended without unanimity.
- https://www.strategy.bg/index.php/bg/pris/legal-information/postanovleniia/171372
- https://bnrnews.bg/horizont/post/552973/darzhaven-vestnik-publikuva-postanovlenieto-na-ms-za-minimalnata-zaplata

**МРЗ / below the formula ceiling.** The adopted €660 sits €12.84 (1.9%) under the €672.84 upper bound of the new statutory range — the government took the conservative option, and the figure also anchors subsidised pay under the Employment Incentive Act.
- https://www.dunavmost.com/novini/obnarodvaha-noviya-razmer-na-minimalnata-zaplata
- https://business.dir.bg/ikonomika/pravitelstvoto-reshi-660-evro-minimalna-zaplata-za-2027-godina

**Осигурителен доход / ceiling.** Presenting the 2027 social-security budget on 4 Nov, Efremova confirmed the maximum insurable income rising to €2,400 from 1 Jan 2027 plus higher minimum insurance thresholds by profession ("as much as people and business can carry"), and conceded this still will not close the pensions deficit.
- https://www.marica.bg/biznes-zona/vdigat-maychinstvoto-prez-vtorata-godina-na-450-evro-eto-ot-koga
- https://www.standartnews.com/balgariya-obshtestvo/obrat-s-maychinstvoto-novi-osigurovki-golemite-promeni-652164.html

**Осигурителен доход / maternity and pensions.** Same 4 Nov presentation: second-year maternity allowance up from €398 to €450 a month from 1 Jan 2027 (75% payable on early return to work), pensions indexed 1 July 2027 under the "Swiss rule" (50% of insurable-income growth + 50% of CPI) for pensions awarded up to end-2026, and Efremova denied any talks on diverting second-pillar contributions to the state scheme.
- https://www.plovdiv24.bg/novini/Bylgaria/Vdigat-maichinstvoto-prez-vtorata-godina-na-450-evro-osuvremenyavat-pensiite-po-shveicarskoto-pravilo-3051136
- https://www.standartnews.com/balgariya-obshtestvo/obrat-s-maychinstvoto-novi-osigurovki-golemite-promeni-652164.html

**ЗЧРБ / foreign-worker law.** A 3 Nov fact-check of the "240,000 foreign workers this year" claim: NRA logged 50,629 employment contracts with foreigners to end-August (45,053 with third-country nationals), the Employment Agency granted labour-market access to 44,496 third-country nationals by 25 Sep, and Interior recorded 44,435 residence permits (12,786 long-term: 9,098 single permits, 2,747 seasonal, 703 EU Blue Cards, 238 intra-corporate transfers); September's facilitations implementing EU Directive 2024/1233 let a laid-off migrant register as unemployed without losing residence, with an employer switch only after six months.

**Държавен вестник / State Gazette.** The only in-window Gazette item that moves money is exactly the MRSZ ordinance: ПМС № 235/2026 in issue 100 on 3 Nov (the same issue's ЦИК decision № 441/2026 declaring the president and vice-president elected has no fiscal effect).

**Extra.** Labour-inspection blow-out: the Chief Labour Inspectorate's 10-month report (out 3 Nov, reported 4 Nov) lists 153,655 labour-law breaches found in 40,478 checks — 2,944 people working with no employment contract, 81,448 health-and-safety breaches, 8,449 penal acts worth over €6M, 836 sites/machines stopped and 109 people removed from work.

**Extra.** Energy-cost pressure is the cross-topic story: BCC and KNSB demanded an urgent Council of Ministers meeting on 3 Nov over non-household electricity (exchange prices up more than 33% in months, averaging €221/MWh in October) and gas above €58/MWh, with business compensation still scheduled only for June 2027; GERB-SDS tabled a same-day draft for monthly compensation of 100% of electricity above €92/MWh plus support for the ~255,000 gas-heating households, to be funded from the Energy System Security Fund.
