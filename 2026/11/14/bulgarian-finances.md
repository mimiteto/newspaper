# Bulgaria — economic & labour-law news, last 2 days (13–14 Nov 2026)

**ЗЧРБ / labour migration.** The government bill published in ДВ бр. 46 makes an employer compensate a foreign worker whose access to the labour market is withdrawn through the employer's fault, requires a written rental contract for housing an employer provides to seasonal workers, extends official passports to unmarried children up to 25 of staff posted abroad without diplomatic rank, and shortens ordinary document processing from 30 to 20 days; the interior committee took it at second reading on 12 Nov, plenary readings still pending.

https://bnrnews.bg/en/post/557423/bulgarian-parliamentary-committee-backs-changes-to-foreign-worker-rules
https://dv.parliament.bg/DVWeb/materiali.faces?idObj=386

**Закона за личната помощ — оттегляне.** On 13 Nov the government agreed to withdraw the Progressive Bulgaria bill (tabled 10 Nov) amending the Personal Assistance Act after protests outside the National Assembly and a meeting at the Finance Ministry with Donev, Efremova and Manolova; the drafts would have ended personal assistants' employment contracts in favour of a service contract, made only pension/disability insurance mandatory (sick-leave and maternity optional, unemployment insurance dropped entirely) and frozen the hourly rate at the 2026 level of 5.24 EUR instead of the 5.57 EUR implied by the new minimum wage.

https://www.bgnes.bg/pravitelstvoto-otteglq-spornite-promeni-v-zakona-za-lichnata-pomosht

**Кодекс на труда — трудов стаж в часове.** Coverage on 13 Nov reported the majority re-tabling the calculation of labour service in hours (4 worked hours under an 8-hour schedule would accrue half a day of service), which affects roughly 280,000 people on part-time contracts and would shrink annual leave, additional leave, seniority classes and service-linked compensation — the same method the Constitutional Court voided in decision №17 of 20 Oct 2026; ombudsman and BSP criticism followed on 12–14 Nov.

https://www.actualno.com/society/vypreki-che-ks-gi-sprja-vlastta-otnovo-vnasja-promenite-za-namalenija-trudov-staj-i-otpusk-za-raboteshtite-na-4-chasa-news_2662404.html
https://fakti.bg/bulgaria/1092408-ks-zapazi-staja-i-otpuskite-na-raboteshtite-na-4-chasa

**ДДС / праг за регистрация.** KPMG's 13 Nov analysis of the Council of Ministers' ЗИД на ЗДДС (submitted to the National Assembly on 5 Nov) confirms the mandatory registration threshold rising in three steps from the current 51,130 EUR to 60,000 EUR on 1 Jan 2027, 70,000 EUR in 2028 and 75,000 EUR in 2029, mandatory structured e-invoicing plus real-time reporting from 1 Jul 2028 (purchase/sales ledgers scrapped, pre-populated returns, invoice-issuance deadline extended from 5 to 10 days, zero rate for defence goods under SAFE, call-off stock abolished from 1 Jul 2029); parliament passed it on first reading on 12 Nov with 122 for, 49 against and 18 abstaining, rejecting 85,000 EUR alternatives from Democratic Bulgaria and Vazrazhdane.

https://kpmg.com/bg/en/insights/2026/11/-proposed-amendments-to-the-vat-act.html
https://www.vesti.bg/pari/po-visok-prag-za-dds-i-e-fakturi-ot-2028-g-6276066

**МРЗ / максимален осигурителен доход.** Carried through the 13–14 Nov round-ups (FlashNews republished the Vesti.bg tax-package explainer on 14 Nov; Ruse News restated the DОО draft on 13 Nov): the 660 EUR minimum wage from 1 Jan 2027 was restated as settled (Council of Ministers decree of 29 Oct, promulgated 3 Nov; +39.80 EUR or 6.4% on 620.20 EUR, 3.98 EUR/hour, ~462,000 workers affected, ~512 EUR net) while the draft DОО budget still has to carry the maximum insured income from 2,300 to 2,400 EUR, the parameter that unions and employers contest.

**Социални мерки по Budget 2027.** Re-reported on 13 Nov from the draft budgets: minimum daily unemployment benefit 9.21 → 13 EUR and maximum 54.78 → 60.76 EUR, second-year maternity allowance 398.81 → 450 EUR, the poverty line 390 → 443 EUR, and a ~7.8% indexation of pensions from 1 Jul 2027 lifting the minimum pension from 347.51 to 374.62 EUR and the cap from 1,738.40 to 1,874 EUR.

**Тристранен съвет / работодатели.** The Bulgarian Chamber of Commerce and Industry said on 13 Nov, after the 12 Nov National Council for Tripartite Cooperation session, that it backs the three draft budgets in principle but refuses the one-off 33% excess-profit tax and the sharply higher sanctions around the euro changeover, and called the planned 5,000 budget-sector posts cut inadequate against more than 12,000 already-vacant establishment positions.

https://www.bcci.bg/news/21974

**Местни данъци / актуализация на оценките.** NSORB's position published on 14 Nov on the ЗМДТ amendments (first reading in the budget committee on 10 Nov, 13 for and 8 abstentions) flags the phased revaluation of base property tax values — +30% in 2027 and +35% in each of 2028 and 2029 — with an expected municipal revenue effect of 73.4M EUR in 2027, 172.1M EUR in 2028 and 270.7M EUR in 2029, and warns the refuse-collection-fee transition lacks a legal basis after 31 Dec 2026.
