**ДДС-прагът: 60 000 евро по проекта срещу 85 000 евро по вноса на „Продължаваме промяната“.** The council-of-ministers bill raises the mandatory VAT registration threshold from the current €51,130 (the legacy 100 000 лв conversion) to €60,000 for 2027, €70,000 for 2028 and €75,000 for 2029; PP tabled between-first-and-second-reading amendments on 19 Nov pushing straight to €85,000 from 1 Jan 2027, the ceiling the European Commission permits, alongside softening the home-raikia ban to cover only sale rather than possession/offering.

**Бюджет 2027 — фискалните параметри, около които вървят всички спорове.** Cash-basis deficit 3.5% of GDP (4.2% accrual) against an expected 5.6% in 2026, then 3.1% (2028) and 2.4% (2029); new borrowing capped at €8.6bn, of which €2.1bn is refinancing; debt to reach €44.3bn or 31.4% of GDP by end-2027 with a fiscal reserve floor of €2.6bn; assumptions are 2.5% real growth, 4.0% average inflation (2.6% year-end), compensation per employee +6.2% and unemployment ~3.4%. Finance Minister Galab Donev called it „фискален реализъм“ under the EU excessive-deficit procedure; Standard & Poor's moved the outlook from positive to stable, which he attributed partly to Eurostat revising the 2025 deficit from 3% to 4% of GDP and to Audit Office irregularities.

**МРЗ 660 евро от 1 януари 2027 г. — с 6,4% повече.** The projected minimum wage is €660 a month from 1 Jan 2027 (€680 in 2028, €710 in 2029), and the minimum insured income by economic activity and skill group is uprated by the same 6.4%; the minimum insured income for self-insured persons is set at the same €660. Budget-sector personnel costs rise 5%, teachers +7.5% (€170.5m), while the „Сигурност“ sector is excluded entirely — the single most contested line in the bill.

**Таванът на осигурителния доход скача на 2 400 евро.** The maximum insured income for all insured persons rises to €2,400 from 1 Jan 2027, €2,500 from 2028 and €2,600 from 2029, while contribution rates and their employer/employee split stay at 2026 levels — except civil servants and judiciary staff, whose split is equalised to the general rule with wage compensation. Regional and business outlets (BurgasMedia and Ruse news, both 20 Nov) report employers as reserved about the ceiling step-up.

**МВР: най-малко 2000 съкратени щатни бройки (21 ноември).** MP Stefan Belchev (Прогресивна България, chair of the economic policy committee) told bTV that at least 2,000 posts are to be cut in the Interior Ministry as part of an optimisation covering roughly 5,000 public-sector posts in total, with about 2,400 outside the ministry plus further cuts in state enterprises, conceding some are vacant posts; he also said the reported ~6,000 officers holding ТЕЛК decisions need verification by the minister. This sits on top of the bill's own measure cutting МВР and security-agency personnel spending by up to 10% from 1 Jan 2027.

**Полицайите обявиха протест за 28 ноември — и отреха да са уведомявани за съкращенията (21 ноември).** СФСМВР chair Iliya Kuzmanov, speaking after the union's national meeting in Veliko Tarnovo, confirmed action in Sofia on Saturday 28 Nov at 12:00, jointly with the prison staff union, calling it a protest „за чест и достойнство“ rather than a pay protest — the offence, he said, is that security was dropped from the promised uniform income approach, and he argued a 5% rise would cost about €100m against a deficit discussed at €4.7bn. He also stated the federation has received no official or unofficial information on the scope, number or method of the announced cuts, and doubted a decision exists.

**„Подкрепа“: щампетни едномесечни стачки в седем институции от 23 ноември.** The administrative-staff union's relay of one-hour effective strikes starts Monday 23 Nov at the Social Assistance Agency (10:00–11:00), then the Employment Agency (24 Nov), the Labour Inspectorate EXEC (25 Nov), the Registry Agency (26 Nov, hour TBC), the Geodesy/Cartography/Cadastre Agency (27 Nov), the National Statistical Institute (30 Nov) and the National Social Security Institute (1 Dec). **Лична помощ и ТЕЛК: промените отложени, пакет подготвян (21 ноември).** Labour and Social Policy Minister Natalia Efremova said on NOVA that whether the Personal Assistance Act is amended will only become clear in the first half of 2027, after broad consultation, weeks after mothers of children with disabilities and personal assistants forced the withdrawal of a previously tabled bill. She framed the package as better targeting rather than removal of rights — including whether a ТЕЛК decision should remain the sole criterion for support, tighter control over ТЕЛК decisions jointly with МВР, and automated cross-checks that will not replace medical judgement. Scale of the scheme: over 100,000 personal assistants caring for more than 85,000 people with disabilities. A separate initiative is 500 „социални таксита“ for all municipalities, in negotiation with the Commission under the Social Climate Plan, with implementation of approved investments from 2028.

**Майчинство: държавата предлага 450 евро, протестиращите искат 70% от осигурителния доход.** The bill raises the benefit for raising a child up to age two from €398.81 to €450 from 1 Jan 2027 — the first increase since 2023 — and lifts the benefit for mothers who do not take the pregnancy/childrearing leave from 50% to 75%. The „Родители за достойно майчинство“ initiative and „Достойнство 2023“ protested outside Parliament on that demand (70% of the mother's gross insured income, plus 100% of the NOI benefit on early return to work), arguing for income-linked rather than flat amounts. Elsewhere in the social package: 9 of 12 family benefits up for €72.5m, the poverty line set at €443 for 2027 costing €114.3m, and the minimum daily unemployment benefit rising from €9.21 to €13.

**КНСБ: 1 516 евро нетен месечен доход за тричленно семейство (21 ноември).** The trade union's subsistence calculation puts a two-earner family with one child under 14 at €1,516 net a month, up €92 in a year, with €842 for a single working person and a required gross wage of €1,085 — below which sit about 1.5m insured persons, or 57.7% of those observed. Violeta Ivanova of КНСБ said the budget's 5% public-sector rise will not offset energy and fuel prices and demanded at least 10% for 2027 in both sectors. NSS background for the same day: food 29.3% of household spending in 2025, housing 15.4%, transport and communications 11.1%, 42.5% of people unable to cover an unexpected expense, and an average gross monthly wage of €1,438 in Q3 2026 (+10.4% year on year).

**Общините искат още 129.5 млн. евро за местните дейности.** НСОРБ signed the consultation protocol with the Finance Ministry with categorical disagreement (13 Nov, published 16 Nov) and repeated the demand through the weekend: restore and top up the transfer compensating the minimum-wage rise in local activities to €45.5m — it was €40.5m in 2026 and is deleted outright for 2027 — raise the general equalisation subsidy by €40m to €295.7m, winter road maintenance by €5m to €30.1m, and the capital grant by €39m to €294m. The capital grant was restored at €255m only after being zeroed in the 2 Nov draft, and even so 107 municipalities (40.4%) receive less than in 2026 while 98 get a smaller winter-maintenance transfer; the delegated-activities subsidy is budgeted at €4,626.4m against a demanded minimum of €4,913m, a 6.3% rise that leaves the health function at just +2.8%.

**Extra — БНБ: ЕЦБ трябва да е готова за ново повишение (21 ноември).** BNB Governor Dimitar Radev, an ECB Governing Council member, speaking at the London School of Economics' European Economic and Finance Centre, said the December meeting will bring updated projections and data to judge whether energy-cost pass-through, negotiated wages and inflation expectations show more persistent pressure — and that if they do, the ECB must be ready to tighten again, with no prior commitment. He cited October flash HICP of 4% in the eurozone against 6% in Bulgaria with euro-area core at 2.5%, September's 25 bp hike and October's hold leaving the deposit facility at 2.5%, professional forecasters lifting 2027 expectations to 2.6% headline / 2.5% core, and an average new Bulgarian housing-loan rate of about 2.4% versus roughly 3.6% on the ECB's euro-area composite — while stressing „не бива да обясняваме цялата разлика между инфлацията в България и в еврозоната с цените на енергията“.
