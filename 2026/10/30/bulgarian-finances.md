# Bulgaria — economic & labour-law news, last 2 days (29–30 Oct 2026)

Sources: Google News RSS (bg, `when:2d`) across BTA/БГНЕС-feeding portals, plus forbesbulgaria.com, minfin.bg, focus-news.net (NOVA News), bnrnews.bg and ime.bg; cross-checked with web_search (МФ/НСТС, квоти за чужда работна ръка, Държавен вестник) — no in-window Държавен вестник hit on the tracked buckets.

**Budget 2027 slips.** The draft budget misses the statutory end-of-October tabling deadline: deputy PM Atanas Peklanov said on 29–30 Oct it will most likely be presented next week, and that the deficit cannot drop to 3% of GDP next year, with Brussels' requirement being 3% only by 2029.
https://www.focus-news.net/novini/Bylgaria/Ikonomist-Ne-e-realistichno-deficitut-v-byudzheta-da-se-svie-do-3-ot-dogodina-3048416
**Deficit.** Finance Ministry execution data to end-September show a €3.6bn cumulative deficit — 2.8% of projected GDP and roughly half of the €7.2bn planned for 2026 — with revenues of €33.9bn (+11% y/y) against €37.5bn of spending, and some €520mln of the gap opening in September alone; NSI separately booked a €944.4mln general-government deficit for Q2 2026 (3.12% of GDP for the quarter).
https://forbesbulgaria.com/2026-10-30/darzhavata-otchita-blizo-1-mlrd-evro-defitsit-samo-za-trimesechie/
**Excessive-deficit procedure.** The Finance Ministry's 29 October position confirms the draft budgetary plan went to the Commission on 15 October, that the official assessment lands on 24 November inside the European Semester autumn package, and that the EDP opened in July 2026 still frames the 2027 envelope — while insisting "decisions on the concrete allocation of public funds remain within the state's powers", after the Commission publicly contradicted the President's claim that Brussels is dictating the spending.

https://www.focus-news.net/novini/Bylgaria/Ikonomist-Ne-e-realistichno-deficitut-v-byudzheta-da-se-svie-do-3-ot-dogodina-3048416
**Work permits (ЗЧРБ).** 46,920 third-country nationals were granted access to the Bulgarian labour market in the first nine months of 2026, from 84 countries, of whom roughly 14,000 were registered for seasonal work of up to 90 days and just under 1,000 hold an EU Blue Card; Uzbekistan is the largest sending country at over 12,000 people, followed by Nepal and Turkey (Employment Agency data, BNR, 30 Oct).
https://bnrnews.bg/horizont/post/550426/47-hil-grazhdani-na-treti-strani-s-dostap-do-balgarskiya-pazar-na-truda
**Labour-migration evidence.** The Market Intelligence Research Institute (ИПИ) analysis published 29 Oct finds no clear displacement of Bulgarian workers and no downward wage pressure from third-country hiring: about 29,000 third-country nationals held a work-purpose residence permit at end-2025 (almost 5× the 2020 level) but remain under 2% of 2.9mln employed, while NSI projects the working-age population to fall from ~3.9mln now to 3.1mln by 2050.
https://ime.bg/analyses/rabotnitsite-ot-treti-strani-bylgariya-tendentsii-sezonnost-i-vyzdejstvie/
**Extra.** Economist Shtero Nozharov (as published: Щерьо Ножаров) told NOVA News on 30 Oct that the government's own plan-smetka books a 7.3% deficit for 2027 without measures, so the real fight is over the structural deficit and the accrual-basis figure — whether it stays at 5% or falls to the 4.3% the government claims, which Brussels will only accept as durable structural measures.
