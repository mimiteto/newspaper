# Bulgaria — economic & labour-law news, last 2 days (26–27 Oct 2026)

Sources: Google News RSS (hl=bg, when:2d/3d) cross-checked against BTA, БНР / Радио България, Investor.bg, Dnes.bg, banker.bg, Bloomberg TV-bg, Mediapool, БНТ, БСК/БТПП (bcci.bg), КНСБ (БГНЕС), minfin.bg, mlsp.government.bg, strategy.bg, parliament.bg and dv.parliament.bg.

**Budget 2027 misses the statutory date.** The draft budget will be tabled "next week" according to PM Rumen Radev at the Sofia Economic Forum on 26 Oct — after the presidential runoff and past the 31 October deadline set by the Public Finance Act, with more money for defence signalled as the headline change.
https://www.bta.bg/bg/news/bulgaria/1232813--byudzhet-2027-shte-bade-predstaven-drugata-sedmitsa-obyavi-premierat-rumen-ra
https://banker.bg/2026-10-26/byudzhet-2027-rumen-radev/

**Budget timing attacked as illegal, not just late.** Same-day coverage framed the delay as a breach of the fiscal-procedure law rather than a scheduling choice, and the budget's redistributive side effects (voucher taxation, a higher social-insurance ceiling) started leading the story on 26 Oct.
https://bnrnews.bg/radiobulgaria/post/548451/byudzhet-2027-shte-bade-predstaven-sled-balotazha
https://www.dnes.bg/a/1-bulgaria/739918-radev-predstavya-byudzheta-za-2027-g-sledvashtata-sedmitsa-poveche-pari-za-otbrana

**Excess-profit tax draws employer pushback.** The finance ministry's ZKPO package introduces a 33 % one-off levy on excess profit for banks, fast-loan firms, insurers, telecoms, exchange bureaux and food-retail chains with 5+ outlets; БТПП backed the tax package in principle on 26 Oct but abstained on the ZKPO chapter and rejects offsetting the levy against investments.
https://bntnews.bg/news/33-danak-svrahpechalba-po-visok-tavan-na-dds-i-oblekcheniya-za-roditeli-prez-2027-g-predlagat-ot-finansovoto-ministerstvo-1413777news.html
https://www.infobusiness.bcci.bg/btpp-printsipno-podkrepya-paketa-danuchni-zakoni-s-belejki-no-se-vuzdurja-ot-podkrepa-otnosno-promenite-v-zkpo-svurzani-s-danuk-svruhpechalba.html

**The mechanism, not the rate, is the complaint.** Economists heard in the 26–27 Oct cycle argue the way the excess-profit base is computed is inflationary, and the EY reading of the ZKPO draft adds the second-order items: a one-off super-profit levy, changed global-minimum-tax rules, loss carry-forward extended to 10 years (capped at 70 % of annual profit), and higher taxation of food vouchers and private use of company assets.
https://www.economic.bg/bg/a/view/danyk-svryhpechalba-novi-akcizi-po-visok-dds-prag-danychnite-promeni-prez-2027-g
https://www.ey.com/bg_bg/insights/tax/tax-newsletter-bill-to-amend-the-corporate-income-tax-act

**VAT registration threshold: 60 000 €, not 75 000 €.** The finance ministry circulated a corrected VAT directive (ZDDS amendment published for consultation on minfin.bg) that lifts the mandatory-registration threshold from the current ~51 300 € to 60 000 € from 1 Jan 2027, walking back the 75 000 € figure in the original draft after IMF criticism — the number that keeps being quoted in the 26–27 Oct budget coverage.
https://www.mediapool.bg/biznes/pragat-po-dds-dogodina-shte-se-vdigne-na-60-000-evro-a-ne-na-75-000-evro/
https://www.minfin.bg/bg/news/13551

**Minimum wage (МРЗ): 660 € proposed, 660 €+ possible.** Social affairs minister Nataliya Efremova said on 26 Oct that a figure above the drafted €660 is "most likely possible" if budget revenues allow; the Council for Tripartite Partnership discussed the €660-from-1-Jan-2027 ordinance on 23 Oct without unanimity, and the public consultation on the ordinance (ID 12802) closes on 28 Oct 2026.
https://www.dnes.bg/a/1-bulgaria/739119-ima-li-shans-za-po-visoka-minimalna-zaplata-nataliya-efremova-za-pregovorite-i-byudzhet-2027
https://www.strategy.bg/bg/public-consultations/12802

**Tripartite file stays open into the budget.** The labour side is arguing both ends at once — employers and syndicates publicly split over the 2027 budget package on 27 Oct, the same week the confederations are lobbying on both the МРЗ level and the tax bills.
https://www.mlsp.government.bg/nsts-obsdi-minimalnata-zaplata-da-se-uvelichi-na-660-evro-ot-1-yanuari-2027-g
https://www.dnes.bg/a/1-bulgaria/739999-kak-bezhantsite-i-migrantite-namirat-rabota-v-balgariya

**Third-country labour / work permits.** КНСБ held a national conference in Sofia on 27 Oct, "An Inclusive Labour Market — the Bulgarian Perspective", on integrating beneficiaries of protection and other non-EU nationals into the labour market under the EC-funded Labour-INT3 project — the practical enforcement end of the ЗЧРБ/labour-migration rules.
https://www.bgnes.bg/knsb-organizira-nacionalna-konferenciq-za-integraciqta-na-bejanci-i-migranti-na-pazara-na-truda
https://presscenter.bg/naczionalna-konferencziya-na-knsb-za-trudovata-integracziya-na-bezhanczi-i-migranti/

**Кодекс на труда — nothing new in the window.** No КТ amendment was tabled or promulgated on 26–27 Oct: the last change was adopted on 25 Sep 2026 and published in Държавен вестник, while the next bill (signature 52-654-01-97, filed 16 Jul 2026) is still with the labour committee, last scheduled 15 Oct 2026 — so this bucket is status, not news.
https://dv.parliament.bg/DVWeb/showMaterialDV.jsp?idMat=246111
https://parliament.bg/bg/bills/ID/167201

**Extra.** Boyan Mitrakev, executive director of KRIB, said on 26 Oct that Bulgaria needs spending-side reforms to exit the excessive-deficit procedure, pointing out that Budget 2027 is heavy on revenue measures; the Commission's own projection quoted in the same cycle puts the deficit at 3.5 % of GDP in 2025 and about 4.1 % in 2026.
https://www.bloombergtv.bg/a/17-v-razvitie/165975-nuzhni-sa-razhodni-reformi-za-kray-na-protsedurata-po-svrahdefitsit-sreshtu-balgariya
