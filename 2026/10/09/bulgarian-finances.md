# Bulgaria — economic & labour-law news, last 2 days (7–9 Oct 2026)

Sources: Bulgarian outlets (БТА, bnr, investor.bg, forbes.bg, webcafe.bg, paragraf.bg, eurocom, mlsp.government.bg, burgasmedia, news.bg, noi.bg, dariknews.bg), plus the State Gazette (държавен вестник) mirrored at bgledger.com/dv.parliament.bg. All dates last 48 hours unless flagged.

**Минимална заплата 2027 (proposal 672.80 €).** Labour Ministry (Minister Natalia Efremova) proposes the 2027 minimum wage at the **top of the new formula band**: €672.80 gross per month, vs the current €620.20; the figure was reached via the new mechanism approved by the Council of Ministers on 9 Sep 2026 and first-read in parliament on 18 Sep, and it still needs a formal Council of Ministers decision before taking effect 1 Jan 2027. The Ministry of Finance's forward budget is currently set at €660, so the proposal is tensioned against the fisc.

**Критики на формулата за МРЗ.** KT "Подкрепа" chief economist Atanas Kacarcev said (7–8 Oct) the new formula "harms people" and demanded a return to the 50% of average-gross-wage rule, which would mechanically yield **€692** — a figure the government band explicitly caps out.

**Кодекс на труда — колективно трудово договаряне (first reading).** On 7 Oct parliament's Labour, Demography and Social Policy Committee passed, in first reading (12–6), the КТ amendments creating a state-supported regime to promote collective bargaining; KNSБ opposes and has said it will signal the European Commission, while employer body БСК backs the text.

**Кодекс на труда — отхвърлени промени.** The same committee rejected (9–8) the proposal to let fathers take the 15-day paternity leave in parts — it must still be taken in one block after the child's discharge — and rejected easing the hiring rules for 16–18-year-olds, so that stays under the current tighter regime.

**Осигурителен доход — August figures.** NSSI published the national average insurable income for August 2026 at **€1,056.31**, and €1,009.78 as the trailing Sep-2025→Aug-2026 average — the figure used to index new pensions; in parallel, the 2027 budget framework keeps the maximum insurable income at €2,400 (up from €2,300), which the employers' confederation КРИБ is publicly contesting.

**ДДС / акциз — горивата.** GERB-СДС and ДПС have tabled a bill for a temporary 9% VAT rate on motor fuels plus an excise reduction of up to 22 eurocents/litre (they claim ~€0.30–0.40/l at the pump), landing mid-way through the Ministry of Finance's **23 Sep → 23 Oct** public consultation on the 2027 tax package (which also moves the VAT-registration threshold from €51,130 to €75,000, worth ~€1.4bn to the budget).

**Бюджет 2027 — фискален съвет (late 6 Oct).** The Fiscal Council warned that 2027 needs reform-grade consolidation: administrative cuts deeper than the pre-announced 10%, pension-system reform, and equalised social-contribution treatment between civil servants and the private sector; it explicitly flags a sovereign-debt spiral risk if the spending trajectory is unchecked.

**ЗЧРБ — petition against residence-cliff rule (8 Oct).** A civil petition challenges the new draft that would allow authorities to revoke permanent-residence permits if the holder has not spent at least six months and one day in Bulgaria in the period — currently the biggest live ЗЧРБ / residence-compliance flashpoint, distinct from the work-permit regime.
https://news.bg/society/grazhdanska-petitsiya-sreshtu-novi-ogranicheniya-za-postoyanno-prebivavashtite-chuzhdentsi-v-balgariya.html

**Държавен вестник — last two issues.** No new labour-law act in the last 48 hours. **DV № 93 (9 Oct 2026)** is thin — four official-section acts, only the amended NHIS rules (Правилник на НЗОК) of administrative relevance. **DV № 92 (6 Oct)** carried the Civil Procedure Code amendments, a Trade Register Act supplement, Constitutional Court decision №14/29-Sep-2026, and Council of Ministers Ordinance №176 (e-vehicle registration). The Labour Code ЗИД that created the new min-wage mechanism was published earlier, in DV № 89 of 29 Sep 2026.

### Flags & caveats

- No fresh (last-2-day) law on **work permits / labour migration quotas** — the live item is the residence-revocation petition above; watch the Ministry of Interior's wave-2 ЗЧРБ consultation and the Uzbekistan labour-migration agreement drafting.
- Numbers in brackets (e.g. €2,400 ceiling for 2027) are **framework figures** — some are the Finance Ministry's forward guidance, some Social Ministry proposals; the binding values still await the state budget act.
- The Fiskalen Suvet item is dated late 6 Oct, just outside the 48-hour window but included because it is the single most consequential budget item this week.
