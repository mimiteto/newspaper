# Bulgaria — economic & labour-law news, last 2 days (08–09 Oct 2026)

Sources: bta.bg, bnr.bg / bnrnews.bg, capital.bg, mlsp.government.bg, news.bg, fakti.bg, 24chasa.bg, nestlersgroup.com; scan via Google News RSS + targeted queries.

**МРЗ 2027 — proposal for €672.80.** Labour minister Natalia Efremova will propose the maximum level under the new formula — €672.80 (+8.5% vs current €620.20) — to the Finance Ministry; net pay would be ≈€522, employer cost ≈€800. Final decision rests with the Council of Ministers; ~460,000 workers are on the minimum wage.

**Кодекс на труда — first-reading vote.** Parliament's labour committee approved (12–6) on first reading MC amendments promoting collective bargaining, transposing Art. 4 of the EU adequate-minimum-wages directive; GIT gets new enforcement powers over sector/branch-level CLAs. КНСБ opposes and threatens a complaint to the European Commission.

**ЗЧРБ — petition against residence tightening.** A civic petition (132 confirmed signatures) fights bill 52-602-01-29, which would strip permanent residence from foreigners absent 6 months + 1 day per year; committee draft keeps the rule but adds exemptions — ethnic Bulgarians with чл. 25, ал. 1, т. 1 status are not yet among them. Bill filed 28 Jul, first reading 3 Sep.
https://news.bg/society/grazhdanska-petitsiya-sreshtu-novi-ogranicheniya-za-postoyanno-prebivavashtite-chuzhdentsi-v-balgariya.html

**MFA fee hike — Type D visas.** The Foreign Ministry proposed a ~20% increase in the Type D long-stay visa application/processing fee; published for comment on a government portal (BTA, 05 Oct).
https://fakti.bg/en/bulgaria/1086225-minister-efremova-i-will-propose-that-the-minimum-wage-be-at-a-maximum-of-67280-euros

**Extra. Work-permit digitisation mandate.** Since 21 Jul 2026 the Employment Agency accepts work-permit, freelance-registration and short-term-employment filings for non-EU nationals only electronically, with a qualified e-signature — no paper transition period (reported this week in an employer-compliance briefing).
